# MACD Divergence Screener: specification v3

## What changed from v2 (why)
Part 1: sync with the code (v2 no longer described what the screener does)
- Five setup types now, not one: REG_DIV, HIDDEN_DIV, FLAG, EMA_PULLBACK, BREAKOUT_RETEST.
  v2 said hidden divergences are not detected. That is wrong: they are.
- Liquidity floor is $50M / 7 days (was $70M), so coins like CT get scanned.
- New "No" rule: a stop more than 20% away makes the R levels meaningless.
- Headless mode (`--scan-once`) with alert dedup through state.json. It runs after every
  4H close and alerts on 4H and 1H Yes signals, signals only.
- Rows are one per coin + setup + side, not one per coin + side.

Part 2: improvements (marked **[v3 NEW]** below; everything else already matches the code)
1. Cross-exchange dedup with Binance as the primary venue. Why: you trade Binance, and
   the same ETH setup on 3 venues is one trade, not three pings.
2. BTC regime uses RSI and the 200 EMA, plus a CHOP state. Why: RSI 51 alone is not a
   bull market, and most bad continuation signals come from sideways BTC.
3. Every setup's score has the same structure: 70 pattern points + 30 context points,
   scored out of the points that could be checked. Why: missing OI data currently
   costs a setup up to 10 points, and grades mean different things per setup.
4. MACD gaps are measured in ATR as well as in leg range. Why: in a dead-quiet market
   the 50-bar range shrinks, so tiny noise crosses pass the whipsaw filter.
5. Thin-coin filters: spread, ATR%, OI and history length. Why: at $50M a week the
   universe includes coins whose signals are mostly noise.
6. Freshness and the macro window are in hours and scale with the timeframe. Why: a
   24h macro block kills every 1H signal for a full day, and bar counts mean different
   things on 4H and 1H.
7. Alert hygiene: cooldown per coin/side, "upgraded from Not yet" label, FLIP warning.
   Why: no repeat pings for the same idea, and you see why a coin is pinging.
8. A 15m trigger for 4H signals. Why: you enter on 15m and take direction from 4H.
9. The backtest covers all five setups and reports expectancy, sample size and an
   out-of-sample split. Grade cutoffs are re-tuned from it. Why: 70/55 were guesses,
   and a win rate without sample size and expectancy says nothing.

## Purpose
Scans crypto perpetual futures on 4H and 1H for five setup types:
- REG_DIV: regular MACD-line divergence (reversal), the trough-to-trough read.
- HIDDEN_DIV: hidden MACD-line divergence (continuation).
- FLAG: bull / bear flag break.
- EMA_PULLBACK: trend pullback into the 20-50 EMA zone, then a trigger candle.
- BREAKOUT_RETEST: a horizontal level breaks, then gets retested.
Each setup gets a quality score, a grade and an answer: Yes / Not yet / No.
Divergence is read on the MACD LINE. The histogram is not used for detection.
This is a checklist, not a prediction.

## Universe
- Exchanges: OKX, Binance, Bybit (via ccxt). Linear USDT perpetuals only.
- Excluded: stablecoin bases, and stock / ETF / commodity perps (exchange metadata first,
  then a curated name list as a backstop).
- Liquidity gate: rolling 7-day traded value >= **$50M** (adjustable), measured from 4H candles.
- Most liquid first, up to 400 pairs per exchange (about 300 pairs pass today).
- **[v3 NEW] Thin-coin filters** (each one excludes the pair from the scan; the scan log counts them):
  - Spread: top-of-book spread <= 0.10% at scan time.
  - Open interest >= $5M.
  - 4H ATR% between 0.6% and 8% of price. Below that the coin is too dead for 1R to be
    reachable; above it a normal stop sits past the 20% rule anyway.
  - History: at least 300 completed bars on the timeframe, so the 200 EMA has settled.
    New listings wait until they have it.

## Indicators
- MACD 12 / 26 / 9 on close (EMA). Divergence is read on the MACD line.
- ATR 14 (Wilder). EMA 20 / 50 / 200 on each timeframe. 20-bar volume average. RSI 14 on BTC 4H.
- Only completed candles. The still-forming candle is dropped, so nothing repaints.
  (Exception: the wave setups may report a signal on the forming bar as "early", see Step 8.)
- History: 720 bars of 4H, 500 of 1H, 200 of 1D (HTF check for 4H), 300 of 15m (trigger).
- **[v3 NEW] MACD in ATR units:** also store `macd_atr = MACD / ATR`. It is used only where
  an absolute size matters (whipsaw floor, "near zero" checks, chart labels), so values
  compare across coins. The divergence size rules stay relative to the leg range, which
  is already scale-free.

## Setup 1: REG_DIV (regular divergence, reversal)

### Step 1: Crossovers
- Bullish cross at bar i: MACD[i-1] <= signal[i-1] and MACD[i] > signal[i].
- Bearish cross at bar i: MACD[i-1] >= signal[i-1] and MACD[i] < signal[i].
- Whipsaw filter: a cross counts only if the MACD-signal gap reaches >= 5% of the 50-bar
  MACD range within 3 bars. Otherwise it is merged with the wave it interrupts.
- **[v3 NEW]** The gap must also reach >= 0.05 x ATR (in MACD units). Why: in a quiet
  stretch 5% of a tiny range is nothing, and noise dots split one trough into two.

### Step 2: Waves
- A BEAR WAVE runs from a bearish cross to the next bullish cross; a BULL WAVE the reverse.
- Minimum wave length: 3 bars. Shorter waves are merged into the neighbouring wave.
- A wave is closed only when its ending cross has printed on a completed candle.

### Step 3: Troughs and peaks (one per wave)
- MACD TROUGH of a bear wave = the lowest MACD-line value in it.
- PRICE LOW of that wave = the lowest wick from the wave start to 2 bars after the wave end.
- Bull waves work the same way with the MACD PEAK and the highest wick.

### Step 4: Divergence (troughs A then B)
- Bullish: bear waves A and B with exactly one bull wave (the bump) between them
  (two in extended mode, off by default). Price low B < price low A, and MACD trough B > MACD trough A.
- Bearish: the mirror (bull waves, price highs, MACD peaks).
- Spacing: MACD trough A to MACD trough B is 8 to 80 bars.
- Bump: the highest MACD in the bump wave is >= 25% of the leg range above trough B.
  Leg range = max(MACD) - min(MACD) from A to B.
- Minimum size (Balanced, default): price (A - B) / A >= max(0.25%, 0.20 x ATR%), and
  MACD (B - A) >= 15% of the leg range.
- Strict preset: 0.50%, 0.55 x ATR%, 22%, and both troughs below zero (peaks above zero).
- TRIPLE tag: an earlier trough C also diverges with A.

### Step 5: Signal bar
- The cross that ENDS wave B. Signal price = its close.

### Step 6: Pattern points (0-70) **[v3 NEW: rescaled to the common score, see Scoring]**
| Component | Points |
|---|---|
| Price difference A to B in ATR (capped at 1.0 ATR) | 0-12 |
| MACD trough improvement, % of leg range (capped at 50%) | 0-12 |
| Bump height above the 25% minimum (capped at 75%) | 0-8 |
| Both troughs on the classic side of zero | 10 |
| Volume over wave B below wave A (sellers drying up) | 10 |
| Rejection wick at price extreme B: wick >= 50% of the candle range | 10 |
| Triple divergence | 8 |
Context (HTF, OI/funding, BTC regime) comes from the common context block.

## Setup 2: HIDDEN_DIV (hidden divergence, continuation)
- Same crossovers, waves and troughs as REG_DIV.
- Bullish: neighbouring bear waves A and B, one bump between them. Price low B HIGHER
  than A, and MACD trough B LOWER than A.
- Trend filter at the signal bar: close above the 200 EMA and 50 EMA above 200 EMA (mirror for bearish).
- Same spacing (8-80 bars), bump (>= 25%, measured above trough A) and Balanced/Strict size rules,
  with the price difference measured B above A.
- Signal bar: the cross that ends wave B. Structure target: the highest high of the bump.
- Pattern points (0-70): trend strength (50 vs 200 EMA spread in ATR, 200 EMA slope over 20 bars) 0-20,
  size (price in ATR, MACD fraction) 0-18, MACD reset toward zero 0-17, volume (B lighter than A,
  signal bar above average) 0-15.

## Setup 3: FLAG (bull / bear flag)
- Pole: >= 2 x ATR within <= 8 bars.
- Flag: 3-15 bars, retraces <= 50% of the pole, does not exceed the pole top by more than 0.1 ATR,
  closes stay on the 20 EMA side (0.1 ATR slack), average volume below the pole's.
- Channel: least-squares lines on the flag highs and lows. Mid slope between -0.35 and +0.05 ATR/bar
  (bull frame), and the two slopes within 0.25 ATR/bar of each other (roughly parallel).
- Signal: the first close beyond the channel's upper line (lower line for bear).
- Stop reference: beyond the flag's opposite extreme. Structure target: the measured move (pole length).
- Pattern points (0-70): pole size and speed 0-18, tightness 0-16, volume drop in the flag 0-16,
  close above the 200 EMA 10, 50 EMA above 200 EMA 10.

## Setup 4: EMA_PULLBACK (tightened, Nov 20)
Bullish (bearish is the mirror):
- Trend: 20 EMA > 50 EMA > 200 EMA, close above the 200 EMA, and the 50 EMA higher than 10 bars ago.
- Prior push: at least one low above the 20 EMA in the 15 bars before the pullback (no chop).
- Pullback: a wick into the 20-50 EMA zone, staying above the 200 EMA, that retraced >= 0.8 x ATR from
  the impulse high (highest high in the 20 bars before it). No close more than 0.25 ATR through the 50 EMA.
- Trigger (within 5 bars of the last touch): a green candle with body >= 50% of its range, closing
  above the prior candle's high and above the 20 EMA, on volume >= 1.0 x the 20-bar average.
- MACD: the MACD line crossed above its signal on the trigger bar or up to 2 bars before, and the
  MACD line is above zero (below zero for bearish).
- Spacing: at most one signal per 10 bars per coin / side / timeframe.
- Only grade A can be Yes. B is "Not yet (watch)".
- Stop reference: beyond the pullback low by 0.25 ATR. Structure target: the impulse high.
- Pattern points (0-70): 50 EMA slope 0-10, EMA spread 0-7, depth inside the zone 6-14, rejection wick 0-10,
  trigger volume 0-7, lighter pullback volume 7, cross on the trigger bar itself 7, pullback size 0-8.

## Setup 5: BREAKOUT_RETEST
- Level: >= 2 confirmed 3/3 pivot highs within 0.3 ATR of each other inside the last 120 bars,
  first touch >= 20 bars before the break. No close above the level before the break. ATR contracting
  into the break (ATR before the break below the base average).
- Breakout: a close beyond the level on >= 1.5 x the 20-bar volume, closing in the outer 25% of its range.
- Retest: within 10 bars, price comes back within 0.5 ATR of the level and closes on the breakout side.
  A close back through the level kills the setup.
- Signal: the retest close. A breakout not yet retested is shown as "Not yet (wait for retest)".
- Stop reference: the lower of level - 0.5 ATR and the retest low - 0.25 ATR. Structure target: level + base height.
- Pattern points (0-70): touches (2 = 8, 3 = 12, 4+ = 16) 0-16, ATR contraction 0-12, breakout volume 4-12,
  retest quality (close in upper half, tight to the level) 0-30.

## Scoring (all setups) **[v3 NEW]**
Score = pattern points (0-70, per setup above) + context points (0-30, same for every setup).

| Context component | Points |
|---|---|
| Higher timeframe agrees: 1H needs the 4H MACD line rising; 4H needs the 1D MACD rising (falling for bearish) | 10 |
| OI / funding: reversals (REG_DIV) want OI falling into B with funding against the crowd; continuations (the other four) want OI rising on the break | 10 |
| BTC regime agrees with the side (see Regime) | 10 |
- Penalty: -10 when OI rose more than 5% into B against a REG_DIV signal with funding on the crowded side.
- Missing data: if OI, funding or HTF data is unavailable, that component is removed and the score is
  rescaled: score = earned / available x 100. Tag `PARTIAL DATA`. Why: today a coin with no OI history
  loses 10 points for our data gap, not for its chart.
- Grades: A >= 70, B 55-69, C < 55 until the backtest re-tunes them (see Backtest).
- Why the common split: today HIDDEN_DIV and EMA_PULLBACK can't earn OI points but FLAG can earn 15, and
  BREAKOUT_RETEST gets 15 for BTC RSI alone. With the split, an A means the same thing on every setup.

## Regime **[v3 NEW: BTC uses two checks and has a CHOP state]**
- BTC state on 4H:
  - BULL: RSI 14 > 55 and close above the 4H 200 EMA.
  - BEAR: RSI 14 < 45 and close below the 4H 200 EMA.
  - CHOP: anything else (RSI 45-55, or RSI and the 200 EMA disagree).
- Coin trend: close vs its own 200 EMA on the signal timeframe (unchanged).
- WITH TREND: BTC state and coin trend both agree with the side.
- COUNTER TREND: either one disagrees.
- CHOP: BTC is CHOP. Continuation setups (HIDDEN_DIV, FLAG, EMA_PULLBACK, BREAKOUT_RETEST) need grade A
  to be Yes. REG_DIV keeps the normal cutoff, because ranges are where reversals work.
- Counter-trend setups still need grade A to be Yes (unchanged).
- Regime changes the cutoff only; it never blocks a setup on its own.
- Why: today BTC RSI 50.5 counts as bullish, and that is the noise zone where flags and pullbacks fail most.

## Step 8: Lower-timeframe trigger (early entry, wave setups)
- 1H trigger for a 4H signal, and 15m trigger for a 1H signal (unchanged): from the signal bar onward,
  the lower-timeframe MACD crosses in the signal's direction AND a candle closes beyond the last 3/3
  swing high (low for bearish) made since price extreme B.
- **[v3 NEW] 15m trigger for 4H signals too**, tagged `15M TRIGGER`, valid within 8 hours (two 4H bars)
  after the 4H signal closes. Why: you enter on 15m with 4H direction, so the 15m trigger is your actual
  entry moment. The 1H trigger stays as the slower confirmation.
- Pattern setups (FLAG, EMA_PULLBACK, BREAKOUT_RETEST) need a closed candle. No early or forming signals.

## Status (judged at the live price, unchanged)
- invalidated: price traded through the stop (completed bars, or the live price now).
- ran: price already reached 1R or more from the signal price.
- extended: moved more than 2% in the signal direction, but less than 1R.
- active: still near the signal price.

## Stop and R (internal, never in alerts)
- Stop: beyond price extreme B by 0.25 x ATR (each setup's own reference above).
- Entry reference: the signal price, or the trigger price if a lower-timeframe trigger fired first.
- R = entry reference to stop, minimum 0.5% of price.
- These drive status (ran / invalidated), the 20% rule, the backtest and the dashboard. Alerts never show
  stops or targets.

## "Good to enter?" (first matching rule wins)
1. Stop was hit -> No.
2. Already ran 1R or more -> No.
3. Stop more than 20% from the entry reference -> No (too wide for R to mean anything).
4. BREAKOUT_RETEST not retested yet -> Not yet (wait for retest).
5. Signal too old -> No. **[v3 NEW: measured in hours]** 4H: more than 24h after the signal bar closed.
   1H: more than 8h (v2 allowed 12h). Why: alerts run every 4h, so a 1H signal is at most about 4h old
   when you first see it, and after 8h the 15m entry has usually gone.
6. Grade C -> No.
7. EMA_PULLBACK below grade A -> Not yet (watch).
8. Counter trend, or a CHOP continuation setup, below grade A -> Not yet (watch).
9. Extended (moved more than 2%) -> Not yet, wait for a pullback to the signal price.
10. **[v3 NEW] Macro window:** a high-impact USD release (CPI, Core PCE, PPI, NFP, unemployment rate,
    FOMC rate / statement / presser, Fed Chair testimony) within 4h for a 1H signal, or within 12h for a
    4H signal, or one that printed in the last hour -> Not yet. BOJ / ECB rate decisions and the rest of the
    list become a `MACRO <name> in Xh` tag only. Why: 24h for everything blocks a 1H setup for six of its
    own lifetimes, and non-USD prints rarely move crypto.
11. Wave setup on a forming bar without a lower-timeframe trigger -> Not yet.
12. Otherwise -> Yes ("Yes (early)" on a forming bar with a trigger, "Yes (confirmed)" on a closed bar).

## Rows (dashboard)
- One row per coin + setup + side. If 4H and 1H both have the same setup and side, they share a row.
- Within one pair, repeated hits of the same setup and side keep the newest one still alive.
- The row shows the timeframe with the better answer (Yes, then Not yet, then No). 4H wins a tie.
- Order: Yes, then Not yet, then No. Highest score first; "No" rows newest first.
- **[v3 NEW] Cross-exchange merge:** one row per coin, not per venue. Binance is the primary data source
  (you trade there). OKX / Bybit are confirmation: tag `ALSO ON OKX, BYBIT` when the same setup, side and
  timeframe fires there within 1 bar. A coin not listed on Binance uses its most liquid venue and is tagged
  `NOT ON BINANCE`. Confirmation is a tag, not points.

## Context tags (shown only, never change the answer)
- Daily movers: VOLUME SPIKE, RANGE EXPANSION, BIG MOVE, AT RANGE HIGH / LOW, ABOVE 7D HIGH / BELOW 7D LOW.
- OI flow with funding: NEW LONGS, NEW SHORTS, SHORT COVERING, CAPITULATION, SPOT-LED, CROWDED LONGS,
  SQUEEZE FUEL, HIGH OI/VOL.
- Setup tags: TRIPLE, W-PATTERN (bump >= 50%), LTF TRIGGER, ZERO-LINE CROSS, POLE x.xATR, MACD CROSS,
  PULLBACK x.xATR, n TOUCHES, RETESTED / UNTESTED.
- **[v3 NEW]** 15M TRIGGER, PARTIAL DATA, ALSO ON ..., NOT ON BINANCE, MACRO ..., UPGRADED, FLIP.

## Alerts (headless mode)
- Command: `python3 screener.py --scan-once --tf 4h,1h --json out.json --new-only`
  (options: `--setups`, `--symbols`, `--exchanges`, `--state`, `--charts DIR`, `--min-weekly-vol`, `--max-pairs`).
- Runs about 10 minutes after every Binance 4H close: 12:10, 4:10, 8:10 AM and PM PHT, every day.
  Each run takes about 100 s.
- Reports every Yes on 4H and 1H. Alert content: coin, setup, side, TF, grade, signal price, and the venues
  it fired on. No stops, no targets.
- Dedup: state.json stores every alerted key (exchange | symbol | setup | side | tf | signal time) for 30 days.
  A key is alerted once.
- "Not yet" -> "Yes": only Yes keys are stored, so a setup that later turns Yes alerts the first time it does.
- **[v3 NEW] Alert key without the exchange** (coin | setup | side | tf | signal time), matching the
  cross-exchange merge. Why: today the same Binance and Bybit signal can ping in two different runs.
- **[v3 NEW] Upgrade tracking:** also store "Not yet" keys with their first-seen time. When one turns Yes,
  label it `UPGRADED (watch since <time PHT>)`. Why: you asked to see these, and the label tells you it is a
  setup that has been building, not a brand-new one.
- **[v3 NEW] Cooldown:** after a coin + side + TF alerts, the same coin + side + TF does not ping again for
  6 bars (24h on 4H, 6h on 1H). A different setup in that window is added to the next ping as
  `+ also <setup>`. Why: one idea, one ping. An opposite-side Yes inside the cooldown always pings, tagged `FLIP`.
- The run reports pairs scanned, skipped and errored, so an empty alert never hides a failed scan.

## Backtest **[v3 NEW: all setups, proper statistics]**
- Rerun the live detection bar by bar with no lookahead, for all five setups (v2 and the current code
  replay REG_DIV only).
- Entry at the signal close plus 0.05% slippage. Fees 0.10% round trip, counted in R.
- Exit at the stop, at 1R / 2R / 3R (reported separately), or a time exit (30 bars on 4H, 48 on 1H) at the close.
  If one bar touches both stop and target, count the stop.
- Per setup x grade x regime x timeframe, report:
  - n (sample size). Fewer than 30 trades -> "insufficient", don't tune on it.
  - Win rate at 1R and 2R.
  - Expectancy in R after fees (average R per trade), and profit factor.
  - Median bars to outcome, average MAE in R, longest losing streak.
- Out-of-sample split by time: the first 70% of history is in-sample, the last 30% out-of-sample.
  Report both side by side.
- Tuning: pick grade cutoffs per setup on in-sample data only. Grade A = the lowest score where in-sample
  expectancy >= +0.3R with n >= 30. Keep it only if out-of-sample expectancy is > 0 and at least half the
  in-sample value. Otherwise keep 70 / 55 and say so.
- Known gaps, stated in the report: OI has no bar-by-bar history (that component is dropped and the score
  rescaled, same as live missing data), and the spread filter can't be replayed.
- Why: without n and expectancy, "60% win rate" on 12 trades is noise, and cutoffs tuned on all the data
  always look better than they are.

## Chart preview
- Price and MACD for the setup: troughs, crossover dots and A-B lines (divergences), the pole and channel (flag),
  20/50/200 EMAs (pullback), the level (breakout), plus the volume panel. Stop and R lines on the dashboard only.

## Other tabs (unchanged)

## Implementation
One Python file: Flask, ccxt, pandas, numpy, matplotlib. Results stream to a browser page, and `--scan-once`
runs the same scan path headless, so the dashboard and alerts can't drift apart. Every detector has the
signature `detector(df, side, preset, start=None)` and returns hits in one shape. The live scan and the
backtest share the same detectors.
